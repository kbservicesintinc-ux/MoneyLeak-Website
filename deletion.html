MoneyLeak Data Deletion

You can request deletion of data associated with your use of MoneyLeak.

Disconnect an institution

Use MoneyLeak's connected-account controls to disconnect an institution. The service removes the selected Plaid connection and associated stored transaction records for that connection.

Request broader deletion

Email moneyleakhelp@gmail.com with the subject MoneyLeak Data Deletion Request. Include enough information to identify the MoneyLeak data associated with your request. Do not email bank passwords, Plaid secrets, or financial login credentials.

We may need to verify the request. Some information may be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or other legitimate obligations.
