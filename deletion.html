MoneyLeak Data Deletion

You may request deletion of data associated with your use of MoneyLeak. Please review the current Privacy Policy for how data is used and retained.

Disconnect a financial institution

Open MoneyLeak's connected-account controls and select the institution you want to disconnect. If the app reports that a disconnection did not complete, contact support rather than assuming access was revoked. Removing a bank connection and deleting an entire MoneyLeak account are different operations.

Request broader deletion

Email moneyleakhelp@gmail.com with the subject MoneyLeak Data Deletion Request. Describe which account or type of data you wish to remove and provide a safe method for us to contact you. Do not send bank passwords, account access tokens, complete card numbers or other financial login credentials.

Verification and processing

To prevent another person from deleting your financial records, we may require additional evidence that you control the relevant MoneyLeak account. An email address alone may not be enough, particularly for earlier anonymous bank-linked accounts. We will explain when identity verification or account recovery is needed. We will not claim deletion is complete while a provider disconnection is still unresolved.

Backups and exceptions

Information removed from active records can remain in historical recovery copies until their retention period expires. Our current hosted database offers three-day point-in-time recovery; separately created exports are retained by the provider for at least seven days and can persist longer if separately downloaded. Some records may need to be preserved for fraud prevention, security, legal compliance, or other applicable obligations. Plaid may retain information separately under its own privacy practices.

Need help?

Contact moneyleakhelp@gmail.com. Never send us your banking password or Plaid secrets.
