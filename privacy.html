Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

This policy explains how MoneyLeak, operated by KB SERVICES INT INC., handles information.

Information we process

When you connect an account through Plaid, MoneyLeak may receive authorized institution and account metadata and transaction information such as dates, amounts, merchant descriptions, and categories. We also process information you provide and technical information needed to operate the service.

How we use information

We use information to provide and improve MoneyLeak, analyze spending, identify recurring expenses, fees or unusual activity, generate reports, surface relevant opportunities, maintain connections, provide support, and protect the service.

Plaid

MoneyLeak uses Plaid to connect supported financial accounts. Bank login credentials are entered through Plaid's connection flow and are not stored by MoneyLeak.

Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers needed to operate MoneyLeak, including financial connectivity, hosting, database, security, and technical providers. MoneyLeak's policy is not to sell users' financial data.

Security

We use safeguards appropriate to the service. Backend access tokens are encrypted at rest. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

We retain information as needed to provide the service and meet applicable obligations. You may disconnect institutions in the app or request deletion using our Data Deletion page.
