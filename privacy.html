Privacy Policy

Proposed update: October 18, 2026 — publication subject to final owner review

This policy explains how MoneyLeak, operated by KB SERVICES INT INC., handles information.

Information we process

If you choose to connect a financial account through Plaid, MoneyLeak receives the account and institution metadata, transaction records, dates, amounts, merchant descriptions, and categories that you authorize. We may also receive account balances, connection status, and information needed to keep the connection working. We process technical session and security information used to operate and protect the service.

How information is used

We use authorized information to analyze spending, identify recurring charges and fees, create reports, surface relevant savings opportunities, maintain your connections, respond to support requests, and protect the service.

Financial connection provider

MoneyLeak uses Plaid to connect supported banks. You provide your bank login credentials through the Plaid connection flow. MoneyLeak does not store your bank password. Plaid independently handles information under its own terms and privacy practices.

Information sharing

We use service providers necessary for connectivity, hosting, storage, security, and support. Our policy is not to sell users' financial information.

Security

We employ safeguards including encrypted bank access tokens and device-side storage protections. No online system is free of security risk.

Retention — how long information remains

  • Linked account information and transaction history: retained while the connection is needed to provide your requested service. Disconnecting an institution initiates removal of its local bank link and associated transaction data. Contact support if a provider connection requires additional revocation verification.
  • Sessions: access tokens are subject to a 90-day inactivity expiration period. An expired session may remain in backend records until the applicable safe-cleanup process runs; expiration and permanent erasure are different events.
  • Short-lived security and cache entries: stored only for their operational purpose; expired-record removal follows the applicable retention maintenance process.
  • Database recovery copies: our paid hosting provider currently offers a rolling three-day point-in-time recovery window. Separately created database exports are retained by the provider for at least seven days, and any separately downloaded copies may remain longer until securely deleted. A recovery copy may temporarily contain information already removed from active records.
  • Support records, limited consent records, and legal/security exceptions: may be retained only where appropriate for support, evidence of consent, fraud prevention, security, or applicable obligations. The duration depends on the specific purpose and requirements.

Disconnection and data-deletion requests

You can manage connected institutions in the app and request broader deletion through our Data Deletion page. We may need to verify that you control the relevant account before making irreversible changes, especially for earlier accounts without a verified identity method. We do not treat an unverified email alone as proof of ownership. We can explain any backup, third-party, or legal retention exception that applies to a verified request.
