Privacy Policy

Effective upon publication of this update

This policy explains how MoneyLeak, operated by KB SERVICES INT INC., handles information.

Information we process

When you choose to connect an account through Plaid, MoneyLeak may receive the bank and account information, balances, and transaction details you authorize, including transaction dates, amounts, merchant descriptions, and categories. We also process information you provide and technical information needed to operate the service.

How we use information

We use authorized financial information to analyze spending, identify money leaks and recurring expenses, detect unusual activity, generate reports, and recommend potential savings. We also use necessary information to maintain your authorized connections, support the service, and protect against misuse.

Plaid

MoneyLeak uses Plaid to connect supported financial accounts. Connecting a bank is optional. Before starting a new bank connection, MoneyLeak asks you to affirmatively consent to its collection, use, and storage of your authorized financial data for the purposes described here. You can decline and continue using portions of MoneyLeak that do not require a bank connection. Bank login credentials are entered through Plaid's connection flow and are not stored by MoneyLeak. MoneyLeak cannot transfer or withdraw funds from your bank.

Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers needed to operate MoneyLeak, including financial connectivity, hosting, database, security, and technical providers. MoneyLeak's policy is not to sell users' financial data.

Security

We use safeguards appropriate to the service. Backend access tokens are encrypted at rest. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

We retain authorized bank information while needed to provide the service and meet applicable obligations. You can disconnect an institution using MoneyLeak's connected-account controls, or request deletion of other associated information using our Data Deletion page. Disconnecting a bank is not the same as deleting every category of account information or necessarily erasing all backups immediately. We may retain limited information where required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons. For deletion assistance, contact moneyleakhelp@gmail.com.
